# Other Layouts

Two layouts besides Canopy and Classic: Terminals, a plain tabbed terminal window, and Canopy 98, a Windows 98 desktop.
Both show one tab at a time instead of the free canvas.

Select **Layout: Terminals** or **Layout: Canopy 98** in the [command palette](Go-To-and-Palette).
Select **Layout: Canopy** to return. See [Layouts and Mods](Layouts-and-Mods) for all the layouts.

## Terminals layout

A top bar and one tab of panes below it.

- **Space picker**: the pill at the left shows the space name. Click it to choose another space.
- **Tabs**: each tab shows its program's icon, name and folder. A dot on the icon shows the agent's status.
- **Tab actions**: click a tab to show it. Right-click it for its menu.
- **Buttons**: the command button opens the command palette. The plus button opens a new tab in the space.

Each pane header shows the program icon, the title and the folder.
Its buttons split the pane right, split it down, zoom it and close it.

Click a header to focus its pane.

## Canopy 98 layout

A teal desktop, a window for the shown tab, and a taskbar.

- **Desktop icons**: My Computer, Recycle Bin, one folder for each space, **Mines**, **Solitaire** and Network Neighborhood.
  Click an icon to open it. Right-click a space folder to rename or close the space.

  A badge on a folder shows if an agent in it is working, blocked or done.
- **Taskbar**: the **Start** button, one button for each tab in the shown space, the agents in the tray, a speaker and a clock.
- **Tab buttons**: click the shown tab's button to minimize it. Click again to bring it back.
- **Start menu**: **Programs** has **Accessories** (games, **Office Assistant**, **Command Palette**), **Agents** and **DOS Prompt**.
  **Agents** lists only the agents installed on your computer. See [Agents](Agents).
- **More in Start**: **Spaces**, **Settings** (with **Layouts**), **Find**, **Help**, **Run...**, **Log Off** and **Shut Down...**.
- **Games**: **Mines** and **Solitaire** open in floating windows. Start them from the desktop or **Start**.

### Office Assistant

A paper clip floats over the desktop. A balloon shows when an agent is blocked or done and you are not looking at it.

Click **Take me there** to focus that agent. Click **Not now** to dismiss the balloon.

Click the clip to read a tip. Click **Hide me** in the tip balloon to hide the clip.
Select **Office Assistant** in **Start**, **Programs**, **Accessories** to show it again.

### Leave Canopy 98

- **Log Off** returns to **Layout: Canopy**. Your terminals keep running.
- **Shut Down...**, then **Restart in DOS mode**, switches to **Layout: Terminals**.
